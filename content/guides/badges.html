Every badge that can appear next to a name in chat, what it means, and how it is earned or given.

Badges come in two kinds. Cosmetic badges are yours to keep and yours to choose between: you can own several and pick which one chat shows from the badge picker next to the chat input, or from the dashboard Settings tab. Role badges are not owned and not selectable; they describe what you are in that specific channel right now, and they appear on their own.

Every badge is a single vector asset drawn on the same 32 by 32 tile with a 7 pixel corner radius, one flat glyph and one colour no other badge uses. Each is shown below at 96 pixels, at its natural 32, and at 17, with a sample chat line underneath so you can see how it actually reads at the size chat renders it.

Choosing which badge shows

Chat shows one cosmetic badge at a time. If you own several, open the badge picker next to the chat input and choose. With no explicit choice, Partner wins if you have it, then your highest paid tier badge, then whatever else you own. You can also choose to show none at all.

Role badges are separate and are not affected by that choice. Banned accounts show no badges of any kind.
