Follow alerts

Shows an animated card in OBS whenever someone follows your channel, one at a time.

Appearance
Sound
mp3, ogg or wav, up to 2 MB. The same sound plays in the Stream Activity tab while it is open.
Use it
The token is hidden by default so it does not leak if you share your screen. Copy always copies the full URL.
Invalidates the current overlay URL.
Fires a TEST_USER follow alert on your connected overlay.
