Questions

Do I need an account to watch a stream?

No. Every public channel plays without a login, without a download and without an extension. An account is only needed to chat, to follow channels and to stream yourself.

Does it cost anything to stream?

No. Creating an account and going live is free, and every account may publish up to 2160p at 240 FPS within the bitrate table. Multistreaming to one other platform is included as well.

What does a subscription actually unlock?

Subscriptions are mostly about watching. Higher tiers unlock watching in 2K and 4K and at 120 or 240 FPS, extra low latency playback, chat cosmetics such as a badge and a custom name colour, larger and animated profile images, and no ads. The exact perks per tier are listed above.

How do I cancel?

Open the Subscription tab in your dashboard and use the Manage button, which opens the payment provider's customer portal. Cancelling stops the renewal and your perks stay active until the end of the period you already paid for.

Can I buy access without a recurring subscription?

Where a pass is offered for a tier or an add-on it is a single payment that grants the same perks for a fixed number of days and then simply expires. Nothing renews and nothing is stored for a later charge.
